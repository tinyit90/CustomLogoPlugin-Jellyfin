Custom Logo

Upload your own images to replace the Jellyfin logos in the web client. PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP or ICO, up to 10 MB. Transparent PNGs look best.

Banner

Wide logo in the page header and on the loading screen. Recommended about 1000 × 250 px.

Icon

Square logo in the side menu, the server button, TV layout and screensaver. Recommended 512 × 512 px.

Favicon

Browser tab and home-screen icon. A square PNG (e.g. 512 × 512 px) or an ICO file.

Options

Turn off to show the stock logos again without deleting your uploads.
After changing a logo, reload the web client. If the old logo still shows, clear the browser cache once (Ctrl + F5, or Cmd + Shift + R on macOS). Native apps may need to be restarted.
